## Why we are writing

Rhiza Research builds **open technology** to serve people and the planet — from electricity quality to weather intelligence. This blog is where we will share progress, methods, and lessons from that work.

We care about tools that are *accessible*, *transparent*, and useful to communities that need them most. Writing is part of that commitment: if a result only lives in a private slide deck, it cannot travel very far.

## What you can expect

Posts will mix short updates with deeper technical notes. A few themes you will see often:

- Open measurement and analysis for **power quality**
- Equity-focused approaches to **weather intelligence**
- Partnerships, field work, and open datasets

> Open technology only helps if people can find it, understand it, and reuse it. This blog is one more way we try to make that true.

## Getting started with a new post

Authors write in Markdown under `src/blog/posts/`. A short front-matter block sets the title, description, date, and cover image; Eleventy turns that file into both a card on the blog index and a full post page.
